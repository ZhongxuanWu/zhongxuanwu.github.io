**Lab:** Xue-Xin Wei Lab, Department of Neuroscience, The University of Texas at Austin<br>
**Theme:** Statistical modeling and the geometry of neural population activity

## Research question
How can we reliably characterize the low-dimensional structure of neural population activity? This project studies the geometry and topology of neural representations using identifiable latent-variable models, which describe high-dimensional recordings through a smaller set of underlying variables.

## Approach
I develop and apply a **Poisson identifiable VAE (pi-VAE)** to study neural manifold geometry. The focus is on robust identification of the underlying structure and quantitative descriptions of its geometry and topology.
