**Lab:** Xue-Xin Wei Lab, Department of Neuroscience, The University of Texas at Austin<br>
**Collaboration:** Eyal Seidemann Lab<br>
**Theme:** Dynamics and geometry of decision-related population activity

## Research question
How does neural population activity in the macaque frontal eye field (FEF) evolve during decisions about brief visual stimuli? This project examines the dynamics of decision-related activity on individual trials.

## Approach
I use **hidden Markov models (HMMs)** and **low-dimensional representations of population activity** to compare two hypotheses: gradual increases (ramping) and abrupt changes (stepping) in the decision variable. The aim is to characterize both the time course and geometry of the underlying neural activity.
