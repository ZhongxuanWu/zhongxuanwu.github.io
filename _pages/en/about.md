I'm a Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://neuroscience.utexas.edu/">Department of Neuroscience</a> at <a href="https://www.utexas.edu/">The University of Texas at Austin</a>, working with Dr. <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/xxweineuraltheory/research">Xue-Xin Wei</a>. Prior to that, I received my B.S. in Biology from <a href="https://www.pku.edu.cn/">Peking University</a> in 2022, where I was advised by Dr. <a href="https://web.bio.pku.edu.cn/UHPB/index.php?m=Index&a=teacher_content&id=36&cid=21">Louis Tao</a>. During my undergraduate studies, I completed a research internship at <a href="https://bcs.mit.edu/">MIT BCS</a> with Drs. <a href="https://weilongzheng.github.io/">Wei-Long Zheng</a>, <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hrI8aH8AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=sra">Guangyu Robert Yang</a>, and <a href="https://halassalab.tufts.edu/">Michael Halassa</a>.

My research focuses on studying the geometry and dynamics of neural representations by developing and applying state-space models, deep
generative models and manifold learning techniques. Beyond that, I am broadly interested in systems and computational neuroscience,
psychology and machine learning.
