About

I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Neuroscience at The University of Texas at Austin, working with Dr. Xue-Xin Wei. Prior to that, I received my B.S. in Biology from Peking University in 2022, where I was advised by Dr. Louis Tao. During my undergraduate studies, I completed a research internship at MIT BCS with Drs. Wei-Long Zheng, Guangyu Robert Yang, and Michael Halassa.

My research focuses on studying the geometry and dynamics of neural representations by developing and applying state-space models, deep generative models and manifold learning techniques. Beyond that, I am broadly interested in systems and computational neuroscience, psychology and machine learning.

Outside of work, I love road trips for hikes and park barbecues. I am also a gamer and sports fan.
